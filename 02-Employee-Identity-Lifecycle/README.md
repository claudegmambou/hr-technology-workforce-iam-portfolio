# Employee Identity Lifecycle Management

This folder contains practical exercises and documentation on managing employee digital identities throughout the employment lifecycle.

## Topics Covered

- Joiner, Mover, and Leaver (JML) processes
- Employee onboarding and identity provisioning
- Department and job transfers
- Access modification and deprovisioning
- Employee termination and access revocation
- HRIS-to-IAM coordination
- Access approvals and security controls

## Completed Project

**Northstar Community Bank — Employee Identity Lifecycle Workflows**

**Project Type:** Simulated HRIS and Workforce IAM Project

This project demonstrates how HR, managers, and IAM teams coordinate employee identities and access throughout the employment lifecycle in a simulated banking environment.

The project includes three workflows:

1. **Joiner:** New employee onboarding and access provisioning.
2. **Mover:** Employee department transfer and access modification.
3. **Leaver:** Employee termination and access revocation.

It highlights the importance of HRIS and IAM coordination, access governance, least privilege, and audit documentation.
